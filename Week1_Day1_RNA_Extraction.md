****# Day 1 — RNA Extraction
**Date:** 30 April 2026  
**Institution:** ICMR-NJIL&OMD, Agra  
**Supervisor:** Dr. Ajay Vir Singh, Scientist F  
**Kit:** Qiagen RNeasy Mini Kit (CAT-74104)

## Objective
Extract total RNA from biological sample for downstream 
cDNA synthesis and RT-PCR analysis.

## Protocol Steps
1. Pelleted cells at high speed 4°C. Added 350µl RLT 
buffer
2. Added 1 volume 70% ethanol to lysate, mixed by 
pipetting
3. Transferred up to 700µl sample including precipitate 
to RNeasy Mini Spin Column placed in 2ml collection tube
4. Closed lid, centrifuged 15 sec at ≥8000g. Discarded 
flow through
5. Added 700µl Buffer RWI to RNeasy column, closed lid, 
centrifuged 15 sec at 8000g. Discarded flow through
6. Added 500µl Buffer RPE to RNeasy spin column, closed 
lid, centrifuged 15 sec at ≥8000g. Discarded flow through
7. Added 500µl Buffer RPE to RNeasy spin column, closed 
lid, centrifuged 2 min at ≥8000g
8. OPTIONAL: Placed RNeasy spin column in new 2ml 
collection tube, centrifuged full speed 1 min to dry 
membrane
9. Placed RNeasy spin column in new 1.5ml collection 
tube. Added 30-50µl RNase-free water directly to spin 
column membrane. Closed lid, centrifuged 1 min at 
≥8000g to elute RNA
10. Repeated step 9 with another 30-50µl RNase-free 
water
11. Stored at -80°C for future use

## Important Notes
- Centrifuge at every step
- Use RNase-free tips throughout
- Add ethanol as protocol varies by kit
- Store extracted RNA at -80°C
