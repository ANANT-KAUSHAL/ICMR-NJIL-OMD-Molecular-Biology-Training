## Objective
Synthesise complementary DNA (cDNA) from total RNA 
extracted earlier, using reverse transcription for 
downstream RT-PCR analysis.

## Starting Materials
- Total RNA: 1µg – 5µg
- Control Rxn: 1µl of Control RNA (50µg/µl)

## Protocol

### Part A — RNA–Primer Annealing Mix (Per Rxn, in 0.5ml sterile tube)

| Component | Volume |
|---|---|
| RNA | n µl (calculated per sample) |
| 10mM dNTP Mix | 1 µl |
| Random Primers (hexamers) | 2 µl |
| DEPC-treated H₂O | Up to 10 µl |

**Step 1:** Mix and centrifuge each component before use.  
**Step 2:** Combine above components in sterile 0.5ml tubes.  
**Step 3:** Incubate RNA–primer mixture at **65°C for 5 minutes**, 
then place immediately on ice for **at least 1 minute**.

---

### Part B — 2X Reaction Mix (Prepare separately, add in given order)

| Component | 1 Rxn |
|---|---|
| 10X RT Buffer | 2 µl |
| 25mM MgCl₂ | 4 µl |
| 0.1M DTT | 2 µl |
| RNase OUT (40 units/µl) | 1 µl |

**Step 4:** Prepare 2X reaction mix in a separate tube, 
adding each component in the order listed above.

---

### Part C — Reverse Transcription Reaction

**Step 5:** Add 9µl of the 2X reaction mix to each 
RNA/primer mixture from Step 3. Mix gently, collect 
by brief centrifugation.  
**Step 6:** Incubate at RT (25°C) for **2 minutes**.  
**Step 7:** Add **1µl SuperScript II RT** to each tube.  
**Step 8 (Minus RT control):** Add 1µl DEPC-treated 
H₂O instead of enzyme.  
**Step 9:** Incubate at RT (25°C) for **10 minutes**.  
**Step 10:** Incubate at **42°C for 50 minutes**.  

---

### Part D — Termination and Cleanup

**Step 11:** Terminate reaction at **70°C for 15 minutes**. 
Chill on ice (2°C) for **5 minutes**.  
**Step 12:** Collect by brief centrifugation. Add **1µl 
RNase H** to each tube. Incubate at **37°C for 20 minutes**.  
**Step 13:** Store at **−20°C** until future use.
